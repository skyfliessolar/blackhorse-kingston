Welcome to The Black Horse

Located on London Road, The Black Horse is a welcoming pub with plenty of space to relax, eat, drink and catch up with friends.

We're home to Drunken Wok, our resident Thai kitchen serving authentic Thai cuisine crafted with tradition — alongside a full bar, live sport and a relaxed atmosphere for everyone.

With an open-plan interior, central bar, lounge areas, fireplace and plenty of seating, we're the perfect place for everything from casual drinks to larger gatherings.
