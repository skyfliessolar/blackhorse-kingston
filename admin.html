Animations & 3D

Website style

Animated uses everything switched on below. Classic is the older, simpler website — plain buttons and no animations at all (the switches below are kept for when you go back).

🎬 Cinematic story ("Amber and Time") — new, optional

Right under the top banner: a film-like chapter where the camera circles a pint on the bar under the Black Horse sign as customers scroll, with four short story panels (the pub, the Drunken Wok, match nights, pull up a stool). At the end, tapping the pint pours their table. When this is on it takes the place of the 3D pint section.

Cinematic: lift-and-tilt pour (new — optional)

Only used when the cinematic story is on. Tapping the pint lifts it and tips it so the ale runs out of the rim before running down to Book a Table. Off = the pint pours straight down, like the original.

3D pint glass

A real-time 3D pint under the top banner — glowing beer, foam, bubbles and condensation on a Black Horse coaster. Customers can turn it, and tapping it takes them to Book a Table.

When a customer taps the pint

Splash in the face — the glass is swung at the screen, the ale splats on the glass and then runs down into Book a Table.
Pour down — the glass tips over and pours; the ale runs down the page and fills the Book a Table form.

Pour style

How the ale looks as it runs down into Book a Table.

Pub lights & embers

Warm out-of-focus lights and rising embers behind the top banner.

Gold shimmer

A slow gold sheen across "The Black Horse" title and a glint around the logo.

Scroll animations

Headings rise in word by word, cards cascade in, opening hours slide in, a gold progress line runs along the top, and the Book a Table buttons glow now and then.

💦 Splash in the face

A real-looking splash of drink hits the screen as if someone threw it at the visitor — drops smack onto the "glass", run down and wipe away, then "Cheers!" appears. Plays once per visit, and again when someone taps "Cheers!". Choose the drink:

How many splashes

When a visitor arrives, the splash can hit once, or two or three times from different sides with "Cheers!" on the last one.

Card spotlight & tilt

Cards catch a warm light and tilt slightly under the mouse (computers only).

🎉 British festival themes

On the right days the website dresses up by itself — snow and fairy lights at Christmas, bats and pumpkins at Halloween, fireworks for Bonfire Night and New Year, and more. A greeting ribbon shows under the menu bar (customers can close it).

Untick a festival to skip it. You can change the greeting customers see; leave it empty for the standard one. Moving dates (Easter, Mother's Day, Pancake Day, Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year) are worked out automatically.

Changes reach the website as soon as you press Save (visitors see them next time the page loads). Animations always stay still for visitors whose phones are set to Reduce Motion (an accessibility setting — on iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Motion), so if an animation doesn't show on your own phone, check that setting first; and the 3D pint is skipped on devices or data-saver modes that can't run it. The Wok Toss game has its own switch under Game & discount codes.

Online booking

Tables & charges

Tables

Each table can only be booked once per time slot. Give tables an area (e.g. Inside, Terrace, Booth) and customers can choose their preferred seating. They are given the smallest free table that fits their group.

Booking times

Customers can choose any time in 15-minute steps within your opening hours. A table is held for the time above, so bookings can never overlap. Big groups automatically get several tables joined together (same seating area first).

Special bookings & extra charges

Customers can tick these when booking. The total is shown to them as "payable at the venue".

Staff & permissions

Shared staff login — one login for all employees

Give every employee the same username and password. Any number of people can be signed in at the same time on their own phones, tablets or tills. Each person types their first name when they sign in, so the Activity log still shows who changed what.

Employees sign in with Staff login at the bottom of the website. Staff tools open right there on the website — they never see this admin portal. Staff tools cover: bookings, offers, menu prices, pub photos, site text, opening hours, discount codes, messages and hire enquiries. Press Fine-tune… next to a login to choose exactly which text, which days' hours, which menu sections and how many photos a day they can change. To change the shared password later, use "Change password" next to it in the list below.

Add a personal staff login (optional)

They sign in with Staff login at the bottom of the website using this email and password. If someone forgets theirs, remove them and create a new login.
