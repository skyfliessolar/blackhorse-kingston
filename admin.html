Tables & charges

Tables

Each table can only be booked once per time slot. Give tables an area (e.g. Inside, Terrace, Booth) and customers can choose their preferred seating. They are given the smallest free table that fits their group.

Booking times

Customers can choose any time in 15-minute steps within your opening hours. A table is held for the time above, so bookings can never overlap.

Special bookings & extra charges

Customers can tick these when booking. The total is shown to them as "payable at the venue".
